How the 50/30/20 rule works

Needs are bills you must pay: housing, utilities, groceries, insurance, minimum debt payments. Wants are everything that makes life fun but isn't essential. Savings covers your emergency fund, retirement and extra debt payments. If your needs are higher than 50%, adjust the percentages above to fit your real life.
