Compare the snowball (smallest balance first) and avalanche (highest interest first) methods and see when you'll be debt-free.

Snowball or avalanche?

The avalanche method usually saves the most interest. The snowball method gives quick wins by clearing small debts first, which helps many people stay motivated. Either way, paying a fixed extra amount every month is what gets you out of debt.
